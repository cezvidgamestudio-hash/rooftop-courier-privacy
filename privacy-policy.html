Privacy Policy

Rooftop Courier
Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how Rooftop Courier handles information when you play the game.

Information and storage

Rooftop Courier does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game has no account system, advertising, analytics, or online services. It does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Game settings, unlocked couriers, and high scores are stored locally on your device using app storage. They are not sent to the developer. You can remove this local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players. It contains no ads or third-party tracking SDKs.

Changes and contact

If the app's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before that change is released.
